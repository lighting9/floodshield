About HydroGrid Simulation

HydroGrid v2.0 lets you draw any custom bounding box on the map using the Leaflet Draw rectangle tool, or select preset locations.

  • Custom Area Selection: Use the drawing rectangle tool on the top-left of the map to target any river basin, coastline, or urban area.
  • Elevation Data: Fetches elevation matrices over your custom region from public elevation services.
  • Cellular Automata Engine: Simulates realistic flood propagation based on hydraulic head differences.
