About HydroGrid Simulation

HydroGrid simulates coastal or riverine flooding over real-world topography retrieved via OpenStreetMap coordinates and public elevation APIs.

  • Elevation Data: Fetches sample elevation matrices over your chosen bounding box using free elevation web services.
  • Cellular Automata Engine: Water propagates from higher to lower neighboring cells based on hydraulic head differences (elevation + water depth).
  • Interactive Controls: Adjust inflow rates, initial reservoir levels, and drainage coefficients in real time.
